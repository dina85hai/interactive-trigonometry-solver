## ✨ SINE RULE SECTION (Finding Sides or Angles in AAS/ASA Cases)

### SINE-EASY-1: Navigation at Sea - Finding Distance

**Real-World Context:**
A coast guard station observes a ship using two observation points. They need to find the distance to the ship using angles and a known baseline.

**Problem Statement:**
Two observation points A and B are 8 km apart on the coast. An observer at point A measures the angle to a ship at C as 40°. An observer at point B measures the angle at B as 70°. Find the distance from point A to the ship (side b).

**Given Information:**
- Side a (AB - baseline between observation points): 8 km
- Angle A: 40°
- Angle B: 70°

**What to Find:** Distance b (from observation point A to the ship)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"This is an AAS (Angle-Angle-Side) case. The Sine Rule can find the unknown side."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"First find angle C: 180° - 40° - 70° = 70°. Then use the Sine Rule: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B)."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"Step 1: Calculate ∠C = 180° - 40° - 70° = 70°
Step 2: Use Sine Rule: 8/sin(40°) = b/sin(70°)
Step 3: b = (8 × sin(70°))/sin(40°) ≈ 12.2 km"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: ∠A = 40°, ∠B = 70°, side a = 8 km

Step 1: Find the third angle
∠C = 180° - ∠A - ∠B
∠C = 180° - 40° - 70°
∠C = 70°

Step 2: Apply the Sine Rule
The Sine Rule states: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C)

8/sin(40°) = b/sin(70°)

Step 3: Solve for b
b = (8 × sin(70°))/sin(40°)
b = (8 × 0.9397)/0.6428
b = 7.5176/0.6428
b ≈ 11.68 km

Therefore, the distance from point A to the ship is approximately 11.68 km.
```

**Answer:** 11.68 km
**Units:** km
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Mode:** Sine Rule

### SINE-EASY-2: Surveying Land - Finding Width of River

**Real-World Context:**
A land surveyor needs to find the width of a river without crossing it, using angle measurements from known points on the same bank.

**Problem Statement:**
A surveyor stands at point A on one bank of a river and places a pole at point B, 50 meters away along the bank. From point A, the angle to a tree on the opposite bank (point C) is 55°. From point B, the angle to the tree is 65°. Find the distance from A to the tree (side b).

**Given Information:**
- Side a (AB - distance along bank): 50 m
- Angle at A (looking toward tree): 55°
- Angle at B (looking toward tree): 65°

**What to Find:** Distance b (from point A to the tree across river)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"This involves an AAS case. Find the third angle first, then use Sine Rule."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"Angle C = 180° - 55° - 65° = 60°. Use Sine Rule: 50/sin(60°) = b/sin(65°)."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"Step-by-step: ∠C = 60°, then b = (50 × sin(65°))/sin(60°) ≈ 52.7 m"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: ∠A = 55°, ∠B = 65°, side a = 50 m

Step 1: Find the third angle ∠C
∠C = 180° - ∠A - ∠B
∠C = 180° - 55° - 65°
∠C = 60°

Step 2: Apply Sine Rule
a/sin(A) = b/sin(B)
50/sin(60°) = b/sin(65°)

Step 3: Solve for b
b = (50 × sin(65°))/sin(60°)
b = (50 × 0.9063)/0.8660
b = 45.315/0.8660
b ≈ 52.3 m

The distance from A to the tree is approximately 52.3 meters.
```

**Answer:** 52.3 m
**Units:** m
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Mode:** Sine Rule

### SINE-MEDIUM-1: Aviation - Finding Distance Between Cities

**Real-World Context:**
An aircraft needs to find the distance between two cities using known angles and a reference distance.

**Problem Statement:**
An aircraft at point C is flying between two cities at points A and B that are 250 km apart. The aircraft measures the angle at C to be 52°. From city A, the angle CAB is 38°. Find the distance from city B to the aircraft (side b).

**Given Information:**
- Distance AB (between two cities): 250 km
- Angle at C (at aircraft): 52°
- Angle at A (from city A): 38°

**What to Find:** Distance b (from city B to the aircraft)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"Find angle B first using angle sum property. Then use Sine Rule."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"Angle B = 180° - 38° - 52° = 90°. This is a right triangle! Use Sine Rule: 250/sin(52°) = b/sin(38°)."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"b = (250 × sin(38°))/sin(52°) = (250 × 0.6157)/0.7880 ≈ 195.1 km"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: ∠A = 38°, ∠C = 52°, side a = 250 km

Step 1: Find ∠B
∠B = 180° - ∠A - ∠C
∠B = 180° - 38° - 52°
∠B = 90° (This is a right triangle!)

Step 2: Apply Sine Rule
a/sin(A) = b/sin(B)
250/sin(52°) = b/sin(38°)

Step 3: Solve for b
b = (250 × sin(38°))/sin(52°)
b = (250 × 0.6157)/0.7880
b = 153.925/0.7880
b ≈ 195.3 km

The distance from city B to the aircraft is approximately 195.3 km.
```

**Answer:** 195.3 km
**Units:** km
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Mode:** Sine Rule

## ✨ COSINE RULE SECTION (Finding Sides or Angles in SAS/SSS Cases)

### COSINE-EASY-1: Building Triangular Garden - Finding Path Length

**Real-World Context:**
A gardener has two garden beds of known length with a known angle between them, and needs to find the length of the path connecting their ends.

**Problem Statement:**
A gardener designs a triangular garden with two sides measuring 8 meters and 12 meters, with an included angle of 60°. Find the length of the path connecting the two ends (side a).

**Given Information:**
- Side b (first garden bed): 8 m
- Side c (second garden bed): 12 m
- Angle A (between them): 60°

**What to Find:** Side a (the path length connecting the ends)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"This is an SAS (Side-Angle-Side) case, perfect for the Cosine Rule."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"Use the Cosine Rule: a² = b² + c² - 2bc·cos(A) = 64 + 144 - 2(8)(12)cos(60°)."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"a² = 208 - 192(0.5) = 208 - 96 = 112, so a = √112 ≈ 10.6 m"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: b = 8 m, c = 12 m, ∠A = 60°

Apply Cosine Rule:
a² = b² + c² - 2bc·cos(A)
a² = 8² + 12² - 2(8)(12)·cos(60°)
a² = 64 + 144 - 192(0.5)
a² = 208 - 96
a² = 112

Taking the square root:
a = √112
a ≈ 10.58 m

The path connecting the two ends is approximately 10.58 meters long.
```

**Answer:** 10.58 m
**Units:** m
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Mode:** Cosine Rule

### COSINE-EASY-2: Bridge Construction - Finding Support Length

**Real-World Context:**
An engineer needs to calculate the length of a diagonal support beam in a triangular framework.

**Problem Statement:**
In a triangular bridge support structure, two beams of length 7 meters and 9 meters meet at an angle of 50°. Find the length of the third beam that completes the triangle (side a).

**Given Information:**
- Beam 1 (b): 7 m
- Beam 2 (c): 9 m
- Included angle (A): 50°

**What to Find:** Beam 3 (side a)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"Use the Cosine Rule for this SAS case: a² = 7² + 9² - 2(7)(9)cos(50°)."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"Calculate: a² = 49 + 81 - 126(0.6428) = 130 - 80.99 ≈ 49."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"a = √49.01 ≈ 7.0 m"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: b = 7 m, c = 9 m, ∠A = 50°

Apply Cosine Rule:
a² = b² + c² - 2bc·cos(A)
a² = 7² + 9² - 2(7)(9)·cos(50°)
a² = 49 + 81 - 126(0.6428)
a² = 130 - 80.99
a² ≈ 49.01

Taking the square root:
a ≈ 7.00 m

The length of the third support beam is approximately 7.00 meters.
```

**Answer:** 7.00 m
**Units:** m
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Mode:** Cosine Rule

A surveying team needs to find a third distance in a triangular survey area in a mountainous region.

**Problem Statement:**
A surveying team measures two distances on either side of a mountain ridge: 450 meters and 600 meters, with an angle of 85° between them. Find the direct distance across the ridge (side a).

**Given Information:**

- Angle A (at ridge): 85°

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"This is a challenging SAS case with a large angle. Use Cosine Rule carefully."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"a² = 450² + 600² - 2(450)(600)cos(85°). Note: cos(85°) ≈ 0.0872."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"a² = 202500 + 360000 - 540000(0.0872) ≈ 515688, so a ≈ 718.1 m"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: b = 450 m, c = 600 m, ∠A = 85°

Apply Cosine Rule:
a² = b² + c² - 2bc·cos(A)
a² = 450² + 600² - 2(450)(600)·cos(85°)
a² = 202500 + 360000 - 540000(0.0872)
a² = 562500 - 47088
a² = 515412

Taking the square root:
a = √515412
a ≈ 717.9 m

The direct distance across the ridge is approximately 717.9 meters.
```

**Answer:** 717.9 m
**Units:** m
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Mode:** Cosine Rule

## ✨ AREA FORMULA SECTION (K = ½ bc sin A)

### AREA-EASY-1: Painting a Triangular Wall - Finding Area

**Real-World Context:**
A painter needs to know the area of a triangular wall to estimate paint needed.

**Problem Statement:**
A triangular wall section has two sides measuring 6 meters and 8 meters, with an included angle of 75°. Find the area of the wall that needs to be painted.

**Given Information:**
- Side b: 6 m
- Side c: 8 m
- Included angle A: 75°

**What to Find:** Area K (in square meters)

**Three-Level Hint System:**

*Level 1 - Gentle Reminder:*
"Use the area formula: K = ½ × b × c × sin(A)."

*Level 2 - More Specific:*
"K = ½ × 6 × 8 × sin(75°) = 24 × sin(75°)."

*Level 3 - Step-by-Step:*
"Since sin(75°) ≈ 0.9659, K ≈ 24 × 0.9659 ≈ 23.2 m²"

**Full Worked Solution:**
```
Given: b = 6 m, c = 8 m, ∠A = 75°

Apply Area Formula:
K = ½ × b × c × sin(A)
K = ½ × 6 × 8 × sin(75°)
K = 24 × sin(75°)
K = 24 × 0.9659
K ≈ 23.18 m²

The area of the triangular wall section is approximately 23.18 square meters.
```

**Answer:** 23.18 m²
**Units:** m²
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Mode:** Area Formula
